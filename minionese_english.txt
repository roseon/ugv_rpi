The English side of every meaning minionese_corpus.txt teaches.

The corpus teaches some meanings in English (Bello - Hello) and others only in
French (Moka, Grazi, Prego), so an English speaker could not reach those and the
words came out invented. Each line below is one Minionese meaning with the English
words that mean it, slash-separated like the corpus's own "Bonjour / Salut". The
corpus still supplies every word and picks the openings; this file only says which
English words reach a meaning it already teaches.

Keep this header free of the division mark used below - the parser reads every
line that carries one as an entry, and a stray mark here becomes a learned word.
That happened once, with the header reading "French (Moka - S'il vous plait)".
